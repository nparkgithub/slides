## Overview

This Reveal.js presentation introduces Paul’s letter to the Romans. It frames Romans as a pastoral and missionary letter that explains the gospel: God’s righteousness is revealed in Christ, received through faith, and expressed in a transformed life.

The deck covers:

- Paul’s authorship, likely writing from Corinth around A.D. 56–57, and his plans for Jerusalem, Rome, and Spain.
- The Roman church’s Jewish and Gentile background, its house-church setting, and its call to unity.
- The letter’s missionary, pastoral, and explanatory purposes.
- Romans 1:16–17 as the letter’s theme and the movement from universal sin to justification, life in the Spirit, God’s faithfulness, and gospel-shaped practice.
- Practical guidance for reading Romans, a closing summary, and links for further study.
